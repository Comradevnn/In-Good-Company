Overprint, round 2: four refined palettes

Design candidate B ("Overprint") in four refined palettes after the cofounder's feedback on round 1: fewer colours per screen, keep the Moss green and Calm lavender fields, bring in the pale cyan card shade, and drop the blue "you" disc, the yellow person disc and accent, and the olive lens. Only colours change. Round-1 Moss and Calm sit alongside for reference. Scroll each row sideways to compare one screen across all six.

Recommendation, not a decision

Recommended: moss-shade-tonal. Runner-up: calm-tonal

  • moss-shade-tonal answers every point of the feedback. It keeps the Moss green field and the Moss status card on Home, uses the pale cyan shade for the shift and list cards, and rebuilds "you", Jordan, the accent and the overlap lens as tones of the same green: dark green, pale mint and deep forest. Sign in and Expired show one colour plus ink; Home drops from 5 hue families to 4.
  • The cyan shade works better with Moss than with Calm. Beside green, cyan reads as a neighbouring cool hue (sky over garden), and it matches Moss's teal success card, so Verify stays in one green-and-teal family. Beside lavender it reads as a second pastel competing with the field, and Calm's own lavender card tint is quieter.
  • It is also the safest. 0 contrast failures, 0 colour-blind flags, and the fewest warnings of the four (8).
  • Watch: the deep forest lens is close to the dark green "you" disc, so the overlap reads more like a shadow than a mix of two inks. If that matters, cooling lens-1 a few degrees toward teal (around #0B4A3A) is a one-token change; re-run the checker against success after.
  • Choose calm-tonal if a quieter, gentler feel matters more than energy. Its violet "you", pale lilac Jordan and indigo lens make the clearest "mixed inks" overlap of the four. Jordan's pale disc is faint on white; deepening person-1 one step would help.
  • moss-shade is the controlled baseline. The cyan cards help a little, but it keeps the blue, marigold and olive the cofounder disliked. calm-tonal-shade works, but the cyan dilutes the lavender.

How to adopt one

Copy the colors.js export from the chosen variant's scheme.md into mobile/theme/colors.js, keeping the palette as swappable role tokens, and keep design-candidates/B/direction.md for everything else (read its colour names through the mapping table at the end of each scheme.md). For the overnight redesign run: "adopt design-candidates/B/direction.md with the palette from design-candidates/B-round2/<slug>/scheme.md". That run starts from main, so merge explore/b-color-schemes and then explore/b-round2 first, or copy those files onto main.
