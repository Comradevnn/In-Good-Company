Three directions for In Good Company

Three visual directions for the app-wide revamp. Each one draws the same five screens with identical copy: Sign in, Verify your ID, Home, Shift proposal, and Proposal expired. The copy comes from the current app and the people and organizations are made up, so only the design differs. Pick one; its direction.md becomes the contract for the revamp.

Recommendation, not a decision

Recommended: C, Carbon Copy, with A as the safe alternative

  • It is the only one whose look encodes how the product works. A shift is a ticket; a proposal is unstamped because nothing is held yet; an expired one comes back stamped. "Confirm before anything is held" becomes something you can see.
  • It has the best form grammar for an app that is mostly forms (onboarding, availability, verification): labels inside ruled boxes, dense but calm.
  • It scored highest in the critique (31/40, against 29 for A and 27 for B), and it is middle on build effort: no shadows, signature move made of plain Views, two font packages and react-native-svg for icons.
  • Conditions if you choose it: keep tickets and stamps to shift screens and use the plain stocks elsewhere (settings, chat, org screens), so the metaphor never becomes a costume; give errors and stamps two distinct reds; decide whether a stamp word like "Expired" counts as new copy.
  • Choose A instead if build speed and scaling to every screen matter more than warmth: it is the easiest to build and the most systematic, but it reads cool and close to a generic civic app.
  • Choose B if delight is the priority: it has the most memorable screen (the proposal hero), but it is the hardest to build and the least likely to survive dense screens and large font sizes.

How to proceed

1. Pick a letter

Compare the comps above. Each direction.md has the full token set, type scale, component anatomy, states and rules for screens not drawn here.

2. Hand it to the revamp

Tell the overnight redesign run: "adopt design-candidates/<X>/direction.md as the direction contract".

3. Get it onto main

That run starts from main. Merge the explore/design-candidates branch first, or copy the chosen direction.md onto main.
