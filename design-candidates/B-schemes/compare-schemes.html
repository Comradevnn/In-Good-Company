Overprint in five colour schemes

Design candidate B ("Overprint") with only its colours changed, so the team can choose on colour alone. Everything else (layout, type, copy, the overlapping discs) is identical to B. The control is B as drawn. Scroll each row sideways to compare one screen across every scheme.

Recommendation, not a decision

Recommended: Moss. Runner-up for energy: Warm

  • Moss answers the "green but still lively" question. It is the app's own green identity pushed to B's boldness: a vivid leaf-green field, a deep forest ink, marigold for the partner and the Deeds badge, a deep peacock disc for you. It reads as the same app grown up, not a new brand.
  • It keeps B's structure intact. Field, then discs, then ink, with the overlap still reading as two inks (peacock over marigold makes an olive lens). Ink on the field is 6.2:1, the best of the green options.
  • It is safer than B for colour-blind users. 0 contrast failures and 0 colour-blind flags, against 5 flags for the control. After review, success moved off green so "Photo captured" no longer reads as a fourth green.
  • Watch: the olive lens is a little dull against the green field. If more punch is wanted there, it is a one-token change.
  • Choose Warm if energy matters more than continuity: the tangerine field against a deep sea-blue disc is the only scheme that rebuilds B's complementary punch, with the best contrast of all six.
  • Choose Moss Linen only if continuity with today's beige ground matters most; it bends B's no-cream rule. Calm suits a quieter product, Cool is closest to B's own overprint but the coolest in temperature.

How to adopt one

Copy the colors.js export from the chosen scheme's scheme.md into mobile/theme/colors.js, keeping the palette as swappable role tokens, and keep design-candidates/B/direction.md for everything else (read its colour names through the mapping table at the end of each scheme.md). For the overnight redesign run: "adopt design-candidates/B/direction.md with the palette from design-candidates/B-schemes/<scheme>/scheme.md". That run starts from main, so merge this branch first or copy those files onto main.
